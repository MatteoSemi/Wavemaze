# The field guide

You are two adventurers exploring the Spectrum Archive. Cool reads blue and green; Warm reads red and amber. Neutral floors, walls, object bodies and identifiers remain visible. A grey marking may contain information only your partner can read. Neither view holds the whole answer.

Read letters, numbers, junctions and shapes aloud. An inscription in your view may identify a button in your partner's room. A shared screen cannot physically prevent looking across; exchanging information is the intended play style.

## Controls

| Action | Cool | Warm |
|---|---|---|
| Up / left / down / right | W / A / S / D | Arrow keys |
| Interact / operate / confirm | E | Enter |
| Grab / release crate | Q | Right Shift |

Settings lets you rebind physical keys. Duplicate keys, Escape, H, Tab, and modifier-only shortcuts are rejected. Prompts show actual bindings. Right Shift differs from left Shift; different keyboard layouts may print different letters on the same physical key.

Stand on or immediately beside a control. Cream corner marks identify the focused device, and the prompt gives its name. You cannot operate through a wall. Interactions occur once per press. Holding a movement key repeats movement; both players can move and operate simultaneously.

Press **H** to open the guide and pause, **Escape** to pause/resume. Menus retain normal Tab and Shift-Tab navigation. The Restart Room button always asks for confirmation. It resets both players and all room devices; no ordinary movement key destroys progress.

## Objects and boards

- **Buttons/levers:** change a device once per press. Reversible machinery may need repeated operations. Shared operation history records neutral switch IDs, not private lamp readings.
- **Pressure pads:** respond to standing or released crates as described in the room. Do not press Interact to stand on a pad.
- **Crates:** press Grab beside one to carry it. It follows your tile. Grab again releases it on your current tile. You may also push. Held crates do not support pads or balance platforms. A partner cannot steal a crate you are carrying.
- **Bridges and doors:** some latch after success; others need continuous support. Safe islands allow waiting. A bridge selector rejects changes while somebody is on its crossing. A shut route blocks safely.
- **Rail tiles:** operate one numbered slot, then another, to swap them. The rail remains freely editable. Submit explicitly when ready.
- **Cart/courier consoles:** walk to a neutral destination marker and interact to request one adjacent track/network step. Occupied or unavailable routes reject the request. Reset Board restarts that machine, not the campaign.
- **Route selectors:** cycle your own route, or your partner's when stated. Compare private diagrams before committing. Changing a committed route removes its power until a fresh valid submission.
- **Packets:** choose a colour to queue a launch, or queue nothing to wait. Both confirm before the clock advances one discrete tick. Travel takes the stated number of ticks. Local reset clears a mismatched attempt.
- **Phases:** a phase control changes the shared machinery. In the phase maze, both players must wait at safe lettered nodes before switching. Far controls let the first traveller help the second.
- **Program:** operate each numbered slot to cycle R → D → L → U. Both couriers execute the same command when Step is operated. A blocked command waits. Rewind keeps your edited program but restores courier starts; Restart Room also restores the initial program. Goals do not freeze couriers.

Read the contextual tip below each room: it explains the new mechanic without giving its answer. Colour-specific clues and current readings stay in their respective views.

## Leave together, save together

Both players must satisfy their exit conditions and reach their own doorway. One can wait while still moving or helping. Temporary supplies and crate support must still hold at departure. The transition pauses both rooms; the last room ends with a joint completion screen.

Unlocked rooms, the next/current room and key settings save locally. Continue restarts both players in a consistent fresh room. In-room movements, crates and partial puzzles are not saved. Room Archive replays unlocked rooms. If browser storage is blocked, play continues but closing the page loses session progress.

## Focus and keyboard troubleshooting

Click the game area if movement does not respond after using a button. Losing focus, changing tabs or opening a menu pauses the game and clears held inputs. Releasing and pressing again resumes movement after unpausing.

Test simultaneous input before a long session: each person holds a direction and presses their Interact key. Check both movements and responses. Some physical keyboards cannot report certain combinations; try alternate bindings. Browser automation cannot verify your keyboard's rollover limit.

Use a desktop window at least 960×640. Both views keep matching integer pixel scales. Shorter windows use smaller room art; text and menus remain usable and scroll when necessary. Use a larger/taller window for bigger room labels. Touch controls are not included.
